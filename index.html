Khuphuka Leadership Academy is a learner development organisation focused on nurturing confident, self-aware and value-driven young leaders.

Our programmes are designed to support learners at critical transition points in their schooling journey, equipping them with life skills, leadership capability and academic readiness.

Our approach is experiential, learner-centred and developmentally appropriate, ensuring meaningful engagement and lasting impact.

Grade 12 Preparation Paradise Camp

Purpose

To intensively prepare learners for final matric examinations through structured revision, exam techniques, and emotional support.

Focus Areas

Subject revision, exam techniques and time management, stress and pressure management, motivation and resilience.

Outcomes

Learners will feel confident and prepared for exams, improve exam technique, reduce anxiety and stress, maintain focus and motivation.

Why this camp matters

Matric results have a lasting impact on post-school opportunities. Focused preparation increases learner success.

Safety, Supervision & Compliance

Khuphuka Leadership Academy prioritises learner safety and well-being. All camps are conducted at vetted venues, with structured supervision, clear codes of conduct, and age-appropriate activities.

One director holds a Basic First Aid certification, while the other is a certified Fire Marshal, ensuring informed on-site response to medical or safety incidents. Detailed safety, supervision, and emergency protocols will be shared with schools prior to confirmation.
